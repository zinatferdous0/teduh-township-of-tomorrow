Why these streets?

TEDUH ranks every street segment by a Shade Gap Score:

Score = Heat exposure × Pedestrian demand × Vulnerable users × Street orientation
  • Heat exposure combines satellite land-surface temperature (Landsat C2 L2, 30 m) with the unshaded share of the footpath
  • Pedestrian demand is the predicted number of walking trips to schools, bus stops, surau, clinics and shops
  • Vulnerable users raise the score near schools, clinics and senior centres
  • Orientation matters because east-west streets get more sun, and trees there cut heat stress the most (KL field study)

Prototype values are simulated. In the pilot they are replaced by real Landsat scenes, a canopy layer and on-street counts.
